## How to edit (please read first)
- **Only change the text.** Leave the little labels in square brackets like `[home.hero.subtitle]`
  alone — they're how the changes get put back on the site in the right place.
- **Don't edit the `«guillemet»` values** (e.g. «Big City Bread Cafe», «Dec 10–11»). Those are
  auto-filled from the **Key details** section below, so changing one place updates them everywhere.
  To change a date, time, or address, edit it once in **Key details**.
- Feel free to leave a comment/note in ((double parentheses)) if you want to explain a change or
  ask a question.
- When you're done, send the doc back and the edits get applied and deployed.

## Apply page

`[apply.eyebrow]`
Vendor applications

`[apply.title]`
Apply to participate in the «Athens Holiday Market»

### Before applications open

`[apply.before.badge]`
Applications open

`[apply.before.intro]`
The «2026» «Athens Holiday Market» is juried, and booths fill fast. Applications open on «Monday, September 7, 2026» and close «October 26, 2026». Get everything ready now so you can apply the moment it opens.

`[apply.guidelines.heading]`
What we're looking for

`[apply.guidelines.1.title]` / `[apply.guidelines.1.body]`
Handmade & original. — Work must be designed and made by you. No mass-produced, imported, or resale items.

`[apply.guidelines.2.title]` / `[apply.guidelines.2.body]`
Here in person, start to finish. — You must be present at your booth to sell your own work, and stay for the full market — no early departures or breaking down early.

`[apply.guidelines.3.title]` / `[apply.guidelines.3.body]`
Juried for quality. — A panel reviews every application. We curate for craftsmanship, originality, and a good mix across mediums.

`[apply.guidelines.4.title]` / `[apply.guidelines.4.body]`
Bring your best. — Photos should represent the actual work you'll sell at the market.

`[apply.checklist.heading]`
What you'll need

`[apply.checklist.items]`
- Your name, email, and cell number  ((note: the form field itself now says "Mobile number" — want this to match?))
- A website or social link (optional)
- The medium of your work
- A short description of your work
- «3–6» photos of your work (under «10»MB each)
- Whether you'd like to share a booth

`[apply.notify.body]`

## Venue page

`[venue.eyebrow]`
The venue

`[venue.title]`
Big City Bread Cafe.

`[venue.hero.body]`
Our market sets up in the courtyard at Big City Bread — a family-run bakery and cafe that has been an Athens favorite since 1998. Come for the handmade gifts and make an evening of it: dinner, drinks, and holiday baked goods are all just steps from the stalls.

`[venue.about.heading]`
About the cafe

`[venue.about.body.1]`
A locally owned, family-run bakery and cafe, Big City Bread has been baking bread and feeding Athens for more than 25 years. Everything is made in house — breads, pastries, and cakes — with meals built around locally sourced produce, meats, and cheeses.

`[venue.about.body.2]`
Stop in for breakfast, brunch, or lunch, or a weeknight dinner with beer and wine. Coffee lovers are in good hands too, with espresso drinks pulled from locally roasted beans. Dine indoors or out on the patio — the same courtyard that fills with makers during the market.
